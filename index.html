App Mechanics

How to Operate

  • Quick Tap: Instantly flips between Question and Emerald Answer.
  • Swipe Right (Easy): Marks item remembered; plays harmonic sweep; schedules graduation.
  • Swipe Left (Hard): Marks recall lapse; plays rejection sweep; schedules for 10 min.
  • Repulsive Drag: High-resistance kinetic physics simulate real mechanical feedback.

Spaced Repetition Engine

Built on a modified SuperMemo SM-2 algorithm. Each successful recall increases the review interval:

  • Initial Step: 1 Day
  • Second Step: 6 Days
  • Graduation: Multiplied continuously by the card's Easiness Factor (EF).

Cards marked 'Hard' reset interval back to zero and degrade EF factor.

Cram & Early Review

If all cards are scheduled for future dates, select "Practice Ahead" to review without disrupting scheduled intervals, or reset category timers at any point.
